1. Alumni Management
Handles alumni records, verification, and data retrieval.
1.1 Alumni Directory
* Displays the complete list of registered alumni

* Provides access to individual alumni profiles and activity history

* Tracks alumni account status (Active, Pending, Verified)

1.2 Alumni Registration Management
   * Allows manual creation of alumni records

   * Stores personal, academic, and graduation information

   * Assigns initial account status and login credentials

1.3 Alumni Verification System
      * Reviews alumni registration submissions

      * Approves or rejects alumni accounts based on validation rules

      * Ensures only legitimate graduates gain system access

1.4 Alumni Search & Filtering
         * Searches alumni by name, batch year, course, or status

         * Applies filters for fast data retrieval

         * Supports administrative audits and alumni engagement activities

2. Event Management
Manages alumni events from creation to analytics.
2.1 Event Creation & Publishing
            * Creates and publishes alumni events (reunions, seminars, fundraisers)

            * Defines event details such as date, location, capacity, and visibility

            * Controls event availability and registration rules

2.2 Event Directory
               * Displays all events (Upcoming, Ongoing, Completed)

               * Updates, deactivates, or removes events

               * Monitors event lifecycle status

2.3 Event Registration Management
                  * Displays registered participants per event

                  * Approves, rejects, or manages attendee lists

                  * Tracks attendance and participation records

2.4 Event Analytics & Reporting
                     * Displays number of registrants and attendance rate

                     * Analyzes alumni engagement per event

                     * Supports data-driven event planning decisions

________________


3. Job Recommendation Management
Controls job postings, approvals, and engagement tracking.
3.1 Job Posting Management
                        * Creates job postings for employers or alumni

                        * Defines job details, qualifications, and application links

                        * Controls job visibility and posting duration

3.2 Job Listings Directory
                           * Displays all job postings in the system

                           * Edits, archives, or removes job listings

                           * Tracks job status (Active, Expired, Pending)

3.3 Job Posting Approval System
                              * Reviews job postings submitted by alumni or partners

                              * Approves or rejects postings for quality assurance

                              * Prevents fraudulent or inappropriate listings

3.4 Job Analytics & Insights
                                 * Tracks number of job postings and views

                                 * Monitors alumni interaction with job opportunities

                                 * Generates insights for career support programs

4. News & Announcements Management
Handles official communication and content management.
4.1 Content Publishing
                                    * Publishes news, updates, and announcements

                                    * Controls post visibility (Public, Alumni-Only, Internal)

                                    * Supports scheduled publishing

4.2 Content Management
                                       * Displays all published and draft posts

                                       * Manages post status (Active, Archived, Unpublished)

                                       * Maintains content consistency across the portal

4.3 Content Editing
                                          * Updates or corrects existing announcements

                                          * Modifies text, images, and publication settings

                                          * Ensures accuracy and relevance of information

4.4 Content Analytics
                                             * Tracks post views and engagement

                                             * Monitors interaction trends

                                             * Evaluates communication effectiveness

5. Donation Management
Ensures transparent and organized donation tracking.
5.1 Donation Records Management
                                                * Displays all donation transactions

                                                * Shows donor details, amounts, and purpose

                                                * Ensures transparency and accountability

5.2 Donation Filtering & Sorting
                                                   * Filters donations by type (Event, Scholarship, General Fund)

                                                   * Sorts by date, donor, or payment method

                                                   * Supports financial monitoring

5.3 Donation Reporting System
                                                      * Generates summarized and detailed donation reports

                                                      * Supports financial reviews and institutional reporting

                                                      * Assists audit and compliance requirements

5.4 Data Export & Sharing
                                                         * Exports donation data to PDF or Excel

                                                         * Enables offline review and record-keeping

                                                         * Shares reports with authorized users
